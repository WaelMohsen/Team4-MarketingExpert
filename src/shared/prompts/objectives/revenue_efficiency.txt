STRATEGIC GOAL: Revenue Efficiency

PRIORITY METRICS: mer, ltv_to_cac, cac_payback_period

RULES
1. Profitability Health Check
- Evaluate MER as a high-level indicator of overall efficiency
- Flag declining MER as a structural business risk

2. Customer Value Quality
- Monitor LTV-to-CAC ratio
- If below 3:1, highlight long-term sustainability concerns

3. Cash Flow Efficiency
- Analyze CAC payback period
- Recommend shifting budget away from slow-recovery channels

4. Value Leakage Detection
- Identify high refund rates or low AOV segments
- Flag them as margin erosion risks

5. Business Interpretation
- Translate all findings into “business sustainability and unit economics”
- Focus on long-term financial health, not short-term performance spikes

MISSING DATA
- Do not infer LTV, CAC, or MER when not provided
- Explicitly state limitations in financial interpretation
