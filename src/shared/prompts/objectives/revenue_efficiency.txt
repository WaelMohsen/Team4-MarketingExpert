### STRATEGIC GOAL: Revenue Efficiency
**Objective**: Optimize blended profitability and ensure sustainable, long-term business growth.

**PRIORITY METRICS**: mer, ltv_to_cac, cac_payback_period.
**SECONDARY SIGNALS**: roas, aov, refund_rate.

**ANALYSIS GUIDELINES**:
1. **Profitability Health**: Act like a CFO. Analyze if the current MER is sustainable for the business's bottom line.
2. **Customer Value**: Look at the LTV-to-CAC ratio. If it's below 3:1, identify this as a long-term sustainability risk.
3. **Cash Flow Check**: Evaluate the Payback Period. If it takes too long to recover the CAC, recommend shifting budget to higher-velocity channels.
4. **Waste Reduction**: Identify high refund rates or low AOV segments that are dragging down overall efficiency.
5. **Stakeholder Translation**: Focus on "Business Health and Sustainability." Connect marketing spend directly to the company's financial stability.
