Objective name : Revenue Efficiency

Primary Goal: Improve blended profitability and scalable growth

Primary KPIs: 
 -> mer : Blended marketing efficiency 
         3+ → strong
         2–3 → scalable
         <2 → needs improvement
 -> ltv to cac : Long-term acquisition sustainability
          3:1 → healthy
          2:1 → borderline
          <2:1 → risky
 -> cac payback period :Time to recover acquisition spend
            <60 days → excellent
            60–90 days → good
            120 days → problematic


What do we Usually Want to improve? 
   Increase LTV (better retention + monetization)
   Reduce CAC without hurting quality
   Shorten payback period (faster cash recovery)
   Improve MER by scaling profitable channels
   Shift toward higher-margin products/customers

Diagnostic Metrics

    Blended CAC (total spend / total new customers)
    AOV (Average Order Value)
    Gross Margin %
    Contribution Margin (after marketing)
    Retention Rate (30 / 60 / 90 days)
    Repeat Purchase Rate
    Revenue per User (ARPU)
    Cohort Performance (LTV by acquisition period)
    Channel-level CAC vs LTV

Common Performance Problems : 
    Low MER → revenue not keeping up with spend
    LTV ≈ CAC or < CAC → unprofitable acquisition
    Long Payback Period (>90–120 days) → cash flow pressure
    High CAC due to inefficient targeting or rising competition
    Low retention → weak product-market fit or onboarding
    Low AOV → under-monetization
    Scaling breaks efficiency → good at small spend, fails at scale

Recommended Actions : 
 ->Use blended reporting, rebalance the budget, and improve the high-margin mix
 ->Improve retention, AOV, upsells, and reduce CAC
 ->Improve pricing, retention, early monetization, and margin control
