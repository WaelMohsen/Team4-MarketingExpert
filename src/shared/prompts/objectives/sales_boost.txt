STRATEGIC GOAL: Increase Sales

PRIORITY METRICS: purchases, revenue, roas, cpa

RULES
1. Efficiency Filter
- Prioritize channels with low CPA relative to target or benchmark
- Flag these as potential “scale opportunities”

2. Profitability Check
- Evaluate ROAS against break-even thresholds
- Identify campaigns generating revenue but destroying margin

3. Conversion Bottlenecks
- If clicks are strong but purchases are weak, prioritize CVR analysis
- Diagnose drop-offs in the purchase funnel

4. Business Interpretation
- Frame performance in terms of ROI and revenue impact
- Emphasize how spend translates into direct sales outcomes

MISSING DATA
- Do not assume missing metrics
- Clearly state when ROAS, CPA, or purchase data is unavailable
