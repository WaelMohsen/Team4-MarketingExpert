STRATEGIC GOAL: Brand Awareness
Objective: Maximize qualified visibility and efficient brand exposure across target audiences.

PRIORITY METRICS: reach, impressions, cpm, frequency.
SECONDARY SIGNALS: video_view_rate, engagement_rate.

ANALYSIS GUIDELINES:
1. Saturation Check: Analyze if Frequency is too high (above 4-5). If so, identify "Ad Fatigue" as a risk.
2. Cost Efficiency: Evaluate CPM trends. Identify if we are overpaying for impressions compared to industry benchmarks.
3. Visibility vs. Quality: High impressions are good, but prioritize high Video Completion Rates or Engagement over raw views.
4. Stakeholder Translation: Explain how "Visibility" today builds the foundation for "Sales" tomorrow.
