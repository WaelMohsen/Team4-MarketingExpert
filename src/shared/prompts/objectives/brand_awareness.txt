Objective Name : Brand Awarness 

Primary Goal: Maximize qualified visibility and efficient exposure 

Primary KPIs:
 ->  Reach :  Aim to reach 60–80% of your target audience within campaign duration
 -> Impressions
       -> Should scale proportionally with reach
       -> Usually 2x–4x your reach (depending on frequency goal)
 -> CPM (Cost per 1,000 Impressions) Good range (varies by region/industry):
      -$1 – $5 → Excellent
      - $5 – $10 → Acceptable
      - $10+ → Needs optimization
 -> Frequency : Ideal range:
      - 1.5 – 3 → Healthy awareness
      - >4 → Risk of ad fatigue

What do we Usually Want to improve? 
 -> Reach more qualified people efficiently without overexposing the same users 

Diagnostic Metrics : 
 -> Audience size
 -> unique reach ratio : 
      - Target: 30% – 60% 
      - Lower = overexposure
      - Higher = efficient reach
 -> video plays
      - 20% – 40%+ completion = good
 -> view rate
    -  15% – 30%+ = strong creative
    - <10% = weak hook
 -> placement delivery
      - No single placement should dominate >70% unless proven efficient
 -> share of spend by audience/placement 
      Minimum viable: 500K – 2M+ for scalable awareness

Common Performance Problems : 
 -> High CPM : > $10 (or above account average by 30%+)
 -> low reach too much frequency : Reach not growing despite increasing spend
 -> narrow audience
 -> weak creatives
 -> causing poor attention

Recommended Actions : 
 -> Broaden audience segments
 -> test wider targeting
 -> refresh creatives
 -> adjust placement mix
 -> cap or monitor frequency
 -> use short high-impact visuals
 -> test video vs static
 -> allocate more budget to efficient reach placements
